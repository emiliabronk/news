Mary Magdalena (22) of Upper Darby, PA, has started a social media sensation. In a world full of pro-birth and anti-contraceptive speech flooding conservative corners of TikTok, one creator rises above.
Magdalena has gained a following for her new belief. Jesus is your IUD.

"Why else would it look like a cross? God literally made the world in his image. yeah. Looks like his fucking image to me!"

To her, even with some traditional anti-contraception viewpoints, an IDU does not count since it's in his image. The theory states, if he didn't want it in me, why would he shape it like that?
